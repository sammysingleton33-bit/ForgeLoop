# prop_cart — author review log (D-019)

Images: `previews/prop_cart.jpg`, `renders/prop_cart/prop_cart_haycart_01_hero.png`,
`renders/prop_cart/prop_cart_wagon_01_hero.png`, `renders/prop_cart/prop_cart_covered_01_hero.png`,
`renders/prop_cart/prop_cart_gameplay.png` (overwritten each round).
Rubric order: silhouette / variation / readability / proportions / palette / detail / kit cohesion / tris.

Variants: handcart (red, sacks + crate + apple basket), haycart (blue, ladder sides, hay heap, pitchfork),
supply wagon (green, crates/barrel/sack, draw pole + yoke, driver's bench), logcart (yellow, bolsters, log
pyramid with rope bindings), covered wagon (red, canvas tilt with kingdom stripe, pennant).
Side boards / rails / bolsters painted in the kingdom shutter colour (village door/shutter rule).

## Round 1 (first quick build)
Scores: 4 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3
Problems seen:
- Everything under-scaled: handcart only 3.6 tall, bed floors at ~2.1 instead of ~3; the 5.5 proxy towers
  over every cart.
- Hay load is one smooth yellow blob (reads as a lemon/loaf).
- Both 4-wheel wagons over 1800 (1868 / 1942).
Changes: wheel radii 1.9-2.6, bed floors raised to 2.9-3.05 with bolsters between axle and bed; hub lathes
6-sided on the 4-wheel wagons, corner stakes only, one sack/barrel less.

## Round 2
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4
Problems seen: the hay heap built from separate clumps read as pillows / bread rolls.
Attempts (each rendered and judged): (a) kit thatch heap -> looked like a thatched hut on a cart (rejected);
(b) radial straw tufts -> spiky sea mine (rejected); (c) tufts combed down-slope -> hidden inside the heap.
Final: one lumpy heap bulging over the rails, flattened straw tufts angled out-and-down at the bulge plus a
few on top, two ropes over the load.

## Round 3
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4
Seen: five distinct silhouettes (min diff 0.27); spoked iron-tyred wheels, painted boards, loads read at
40 studs; covered wagon canvas + stripe is the strongest read. Tris 1232-1784 / 1800.
Remaining weaknesses: hay is still the weakest load (stylised tufts); wagons near budget; carts have no
open tailgate/animation (static props).

## Round 4 (final full build check)
Problems seen in the 600 px final: the covered wagon went to 1812/1800 (the reworked slumped sack has one more
profile ring) and the hay fringe still read slightly spiky at full resolution.
Changes: one of the two rear sacks on the covered wagon replaced by a small crate (1776); hay tufts 18
instead of 24, shorter (0.8-1.05) and combed further down the heap. Rebuilt in full.
