# tree_pine — author review log (D-019)

Images: `previews/tree_pine.jpg`, `renders/tree_pine/tree_pine_01_hero.png`, `renders/tree_pine/tree_pine_02_hero.png`,
`renders/tree_pine/tree_pine_gameplay.png` (overwritten each round; scores taken from that round's images).

Rubric order: silhouette / variation / readability @60-80 / proportions / palette / detail / kit cohesion / tris.

## Round 1
Scores: 3 / 3 / 4 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 4
Problems seen:
- Reads as a conifer at gameplay distance, but the upper third is skeletal: tiny pads with big vertical gaps,
  trunk visible between tiers, arrow-shaped leader.
- Pads thin and petal-like; snowy variant almost entirely white; blue variant indistinguishable from green.
Changes: tier heights compressed towards the top (u^0.82), bigger top radius, thicker pads with stronger droop;
leader = 3 upturned pads + spike; snow only on outer upward faces; blue scheme uses copper_green tips.

## Round 2
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 3 / 4
Problems seen: pads read as spade-shaped leaves (heart outline, pointed tip, light rim) rather than branch clusters;
top read as a fleur-de-lis.
Changes: pad loft with a bumpy outline (needle tufts along the branch) and a rounded drooping tip; narrower pads,
one extra pad on the bottom tier.

## Round 3
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 3 / 3
Problems seen: pentagonal cross-section + per-face tip/top colours gave a faceted patchwork; snow checkered;
spruce over budget (4490).
Changes: hexagonal 6-ring pads, colour only by distance from trunk (dark inner / mid / light tips) + dark underside,
snow on faces with normal z > 0.62 outside the inner 30 %; spike leader; spruce 8 tiers.

## Round 4
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4
Changes: smaller, droopier top tuft (no star topper); pine cones under tiers 2-3; bare branches only under the
lowest tier; budget 3.2k-3.9k / 4000.
Seen: six clearly different conifers (classic 40, broad 32, spruce 45, snowy 36, blue-green 42, young 29), layered
drooping tiers with dark interiors and light tips, bare lower trunk with dead stubs and roots, cones.
Remaining weaknesses: pads are still smooth "fingers" (no needle texture by design); classic and snowy share a
silhouette family (snow carries the difference); tris close to budget, little headroom for more tiers.
