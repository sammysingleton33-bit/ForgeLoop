Rubric order: silhouette / variation / readability @40 / proportions / palette / detail / kit cohesion / tris.

## Round 1
Scores: 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3
Seen: six clear kinds (wildflowers, tulips, daisies, sunflowers, lavender, poppies) with leafy mound + broad leaves,
stems and solid heads; storybook colours match the cottage flower boxes.
Problems: poppies were square red cards (4-petal plates); daisies and wildflowers over the 800 budget (1100 / 856).
Changes: poppies = open lathe bowl with a black seed boss, tilted to the viewer; daisies 6 petals x 8 heads,
wildflowers 9 heads.

## Round 2
Scores: 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 4

Changes: mound built with a flattened underside (bottom 0.08) instead of clamping a full sphere; daisy heads
0.46-0.56, stems 1.3-2.1.
