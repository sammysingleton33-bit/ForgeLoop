Rubric order: silhouette / variation / readability @40 / proportions / palette / detail / kit cohesion / tris.

## Round 1
Scores: 2 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 2 / 3 / 3
Problems seen:
- Round bushes = one big smooth core dome ("jelly blob") with the crown clumps buried inside it; only a ring of
  ground clumps showed. Light splotches where buried clumps poked through.
- Hedge read as a caterpillar (row of balls on stem "legs").
- Flowers tiny confetti diamonds; red berries on bush 05 invisible (buried between clumps).
- 3 variants over 1500 tris.
Changes: core shrunk; 8-9 clumps Fibonacci-spread over the core's upper surface (oak crown recipe); hedge = one lofted
rounded-box mass with noise lumps, dark base, light top, rounded ends, clipped to 16 long; cheaper flowers.

## Round 2
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 / 4
Problems seen: flowers looked like little parasols/mushrooms standing on the leaves; berries still partly buried;
bush base lifted slightly so stems read as legs.
Changes: accent points are now rejected when they fall inside a neighbouring clump (only the visible outer surface
gets flowers/berries); lowest clump ring reaches -38 deg and sits on the ground; flowers flattened and aligned to the
leaf surface, half sunk, with a yellow centre on every other bloom.

## Round 3
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4
Changes: plain round bushes spend spare budget on 180-tri tops; hedge side lumps rounder; hedge stations trimmed to
stay in budget (1100-1490 / 1500).
Seen: 8 readable variants: two round mounds (light / dark), pink and mixed flowering bushes, red and blue berry
bushes, plain and flowering hedge segments (16 long). Accents read at 40 studs.
Remaining weaknesses: round_01 and round_02 differ mainly by size and green; clumps still show soft facets at hero
distance; the hedge ends are rounded, so segments butted together show a small notch.
