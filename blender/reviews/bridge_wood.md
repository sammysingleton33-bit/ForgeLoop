# bridge_wood — author review log (D-019)

Images: `previews/bridge_wood.jpg`, `renders/bridge_wood/bridge_wood_trestle_01_hero.png`,
`renders/bridge_wood/bridge_wood_gameplay.png` (overwritten each round).
Rubric order: silhouette / variation / readability / proportions / palette / detail / kit cohesion / tris.

Variants: plank (32 long, low deck 3.4, one centre trestle + ramp bents, timber railings), trestle (44 long,
deck 6.4 on five braced bents with mud sills on stone, rope handrails), humped (40 long storybook footbridge
on laminated arch ribs with spandrel posts, X-braced railings, lanterns). Planks run across (13 long, 12.4
clear between railings); deck ends at z 0.3 meet the ground.

## Round 1 (first quick build)
Scores: 4 / 4 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 4 / 2
Problems seen:
- Trestle variant over budget (4772): rope lashings on every post + two rope lines per panel.
- Trestle deck too low (5.2): the bents disappear under the deck, so it reads as another plank bridge.
- Ramps read as staircases (individually tilted planks with dark gaps); too many dark planks (patchy).
Changes: per-bent stone footing course + timber mud sill instead of a block per post; lashings on every third
post; trestle deck 6.4 with 14-stud ramps, smoothstep ramps; plank gaps 0.08, fewer dark planks.

## Round 2
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 4
Problems seen: 16-stud unsupported middle span on the trestle bridge; ramps still stair-like.
Changes: centre bent + crossed sway braces; two light wheel treadways (longitudinal plank strips) over the
cross planks on the plank/trestle bridges (cart tracks, breaks the stair read); low guard rope dropped.

## Round 3
Scores: 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 4 / 4 / 4
Seen: three distinct silhouettes (flat-with-ramps, tall trestle, arched footbridge; min diff 0.36); bents,
arch ribs and railings read at 63 studs. Tris 2316 / 3496 / 3092 of 4000.
Remaining weaknesses: palette is all wood browns (neutral by design: bridges are shared infrastructure; the
humped bridge's lanterns are the only warm accent); ramp slope peaks ~31 deg on the trestle bridge.
