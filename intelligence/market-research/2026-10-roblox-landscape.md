- **Status:** Input for strategy (Codex / FORGE-00). This is not a decision. Choosing a game or monetization model needs owner approval (`AGENTS.md`).
- **Method:** WebSearch, plus direct reads of Roblox's creator docs. **WebFetch failed** with DNS `ENOTFOUND` on sec.gov, s27.q4cdn.com (Roblox IR), create.roblox.com, rowatcher.com and studiokrew.com, so findings from those hosts come from search-result snippets or summaries, and their confidence is lowered one step compared with reading the primary page.
- **Creator docs (updated 2026-10-12):** create.roblox.com/docs is published from `github.com/Roblox/creator-docs`, and its pages are readable at `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/<path>` (HTTP 200). - **Labels:** **O** = OBSERVED (the source states it). **I** = INFERRED (my analysis). Confidence:
  - **high:** official Roblox source (newsroom, creator docs, SEC/IR), corroborated by at least two outlets.
  - **med:** official source seen once in a snippet, or reputable trade press (PocketGamer.biz, GamesBeat, TechCrunch, Tubefilter, PC Gamer).
  - **low:** third-party trackers or blogs (RoWatcher, Bloxodes, StudioKrew, profitable.app, rolearn.dev, creation.dev), a single source, or conflicting figures.
- **Tracker caveat:** live CCU swings 2–3x between the daily trough and peak (StudioKrew, Aug 2026). The trackers disagree. Treat all per-game CCU figures as snapshots.

## Implications for a small AI-assisted team (INFERRED, labelled)

All points below are **INFERRED** from the findings above. None is a decision. Genre and monetization choices require owner approval.

1. **Build for 28-day retention, not a viral spike.** RFY now rewards play-through rate, play days and co-play over 28 days (5.1–5.3), and Roblox is accepting lower bookings to enforce this (0.4–0.5). The acceptance criteria should include D1, D7 and D28 targets, not only CCU.
2. **Treat the first 60–180 seconds as a hard gate.** Bounce under 60s is a ranking signal (5.2). Aim for a first reward in under 60s, a clear goal by 3 min and no long tutorial (12.1). Integration & QA should measure first-session funnels before any promotion.
3. **Make social play work without text chat.** Roughly 4 in 10 DAU were not age-checked in Q2 (7.3, 8.1), under-9s have chat off, and Kids accounts have communication off by default (8.4). Design for co-op through shared objectives, emotes, pings and party joins, so chat is a bonus.
4. **Pick a rating deliberately.** A Minimal or Mild rating with no social-hangout or free-form drawing features keeps the 5–15 catalogs possible, though Kids still requires selection (8.4). Older-skewing genres (shooter, RPG, sports and racing) match Roblox's monetization priorities and the 13+ ad audience (7.2, 11.8). The two goals pull in different directions, so choose one per game.
5. **Avoid saturated meme and clone genres.** Brainrot, generic simulators and keyboard obbies attract copycats within weeks and decay fast (1.7–1.8, 9.2, 1.12). Better-evidenced openings are co-op horror or survival with depth, sports or arcade sports, and novel mid-core games for 13+ (9.3–9.5, 9.8). Evidence for these is mostly low or medium confidence, so validate with small test launches.
6. **Use transparent, server-authoritative monetization.** The University of Sydney study, the FTC request and Brazil's under-18 ban on paid random items (10.4, 8.7–8.8) make obscured currencies and paid random items a regulatory and reputational risk. Prefer direct-priced passes, cosmetics and a season pass; avoid hard-coded prices so Managed Pricing works (11.7); price passes along the observed ladder (11.11). Paid random items need odds and policy gating per `AGENTS.md`.
7. **Use platform payouts that reward the behaviour we want.** Design a daily habit loop worth ≥10 min (Creator Rewards, 6.2). Offer paid private servers as a group-play feature (Plus payouts, 6.6–6.7). An in-game Plus prompt is optional (250 R$ per acquisition, 6.6). Rewarded video should be gated to 13+ (11.8).
8. **Use the AI team's main advantage: content cadence.** Leaders ship weekly small updates or events plus monthly large drops (3.1–3.6), but rushed events cause backlash (3.2, 10.1). Plan a sustainable cadence, roughly a weekly event and a monthly content drop, with QA gates, rather than daily churn.
9. **Design clip-able moments.** Off-platform video (YouTube, TikTok) starts the spikes (4.1–4.5), and Roblox Moments and the spotlight series exist in-platform (4.3, 2.1). Build memorable co-op scares, reveals and shareable outcomes, and provide creator-friendly codes or events. Any paid creator outreach needs owner approval (it spends money).
10. **Plan a portfolio of small bets with kill criteria.** The median DevEx creator earned about $1,500 a year while the top 1,000 averaged $1.4M (2.6). A solo developer did break out in 2026 (2.1). Run several small, cheap launches, and scale only on measured D7/D28 retention and play-through rate. Jumpstart (2.7) fits a small team; applying is an external action that needs owner approval.
11. **Mobile first.** About 80% of DAU were on mobile in 2024 (7.5), and APAC is the largest region (0.7). Prioritise touch UI, low-end device performance and localization (Roblox's 2024 genre research also flagged localization). Browser and standalone-app distribution (5.7) may reduce first-session friction later; that is unverified.
