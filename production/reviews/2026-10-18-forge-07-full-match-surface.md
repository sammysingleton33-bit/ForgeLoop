## 4. Checked on main and found sound (read, not run)
- **Combat is gated on war.** `atWar` guards `Army.Command` attack and every target selection in `Military.luau` (lines 261, 400, 423, 679-818, 920, 1006).
- **Elimination removes everything in one place.** `eliminate` (`Military.luau:544-607`) flips `alive`, pays the conqueror, plunders the treasury, removes citizens, units and buildings, resets every relation of the fallen kingdom to Peace, and emits one `KingdomEliminated`. `EntityService` despawns the removed ids by diffing its known set against the sim (`EntityService.luau:440-447`), after a short wait for the die cue, so clients do not keep ghost rigs.
- **The match always has an end path.** Conquest ends it when one or no kingdom stands (`Match.luau:569`); phases advance on sim time (`:582`); `_finish` yields no winner when none stands, so a double elimination is a draw (`:510`); Results returns to Lobby with the same players after `resultsSec` (`:615-626`).
- **Joining.** `_room` returns 0 for a dead kingdom (`Match.luau:265-271`), so no joiner is seated in an eliminated kingdom; with no room anywhere `join` answers `match_full`.
- **Argument validation** of the Diplomacy handlers: a non-string or over-long kingdom id answers `invalid_args` (`Diplomacy.luau:36`).
- **The recruit cap already counts queued recruits.** `Military.recruit` tests `#kingdom.unitIds + queuedCount + count > recruitCap` before it charges (`Military.luau:220`), the shape D-055's `army_cap` should reuse.
- **Interest changes rate, not presence.** `Interest.select` sends near entities every tick and each far entity on the tick where `(id + tick) % farEvery == 0`, so every entity still updates (`Interest.luau` header).

## 5. Review checklist for the Crownfall PRs
**FL-0125 (full match by default)**

6. `Match.Spectate`: an eliminated ruler keeps no command rights, cannot be reseated, and spectating survives the Results to Lobby reset. A late joiner when every living kingdom is full or dead becomes a spectator, not a stuck player.

18. The new war and peace remotes: declared argument validation (kingdom id string 1..16), a per-player rate limit, results mapped to refusal codes, and a G8 row at merge. 20. Seniority: the senior ruler is the longest-seated human who is still present and active. Specs for: the senior leaves and the next-longest takes over; a ruler who leaves and rejoins becomes the newest; two rulers seated in the same tick have a deterministic order; the first human to take over a governor kingdom becomes senior.
21. Only the senior ruler can declare war or accept peace; any ruler can propose peace. A non-senior's request to declare war becomes a server notice to the senior: the notice text is server-built (no client text), de-duplicated and rate-limited per requester, so a co-ruler cannot spam the senior. If seniority passes while an ask is pending, the ask is re-routed or dropped, not delivered to the wrong ruler.
22. The 120 s newcomer lock (config) blocks declare war, accept peace, and building move or demolish, and per D-054 (7) counts only for rulers seated after Founding starts. Lobby seating, and seating before the first phase, is exempt; the Results to Lobby re-seating is exempt too. The time source is server-side and cannot be reset by the client. A ruler who leaves during Founding or later and rejoins is locked again. Every route that moves or removes a building is covered: `Build.Move`, `Build.MoveToSlot`, and any `Build.Demolish` or `CancelConstruction` route FL-0126 adds. Specs: a Lobby-seated ruler can move a building at once; a joiner seated in Expansion cannot for 120 s and can after.
23. Everything else stays equal: building, upgrading, recruiting, quotas, conversations and treasury still work for a newcomer and a non-senior, with their existing rate limits; a spec proves it.
24. Idle senior, D-054 (6): a senior with no command or input for 90 s (config) loses seniority to the next present, active ruler until they act again. Check: (a) the activity signal is server-observed and passive polling (`Kingdom.GetState`, `GetPopulation` and similar read remotes the HUD calls on a timer) does not count as input, otherwise an AFK client keeps seniority forever; (b) seniority returns to the senior the moment they act and does not flip-flop between two rulers more than once per 90 s window; (c) if no other ruler is present and active, the idle senior keeps seniority (a lone ruler is never left without one); (d) if the next active ruler is still inside the newcomer lock, the PR states what happens (at worst a stall of up to 120 s with nobody able to declare war or accept peace, which is acceptable if stated); (e) the 90 s is read from config and a spec drives it with the injected clock. Note that `Sim.Focus` reports (item 40) are a passive client signal too and must not count as input for seniority.
25. `Match.luau:29` ("equal command rights") is updated to the D-054 wording, and FL-0129's panel states disabled for a non-senior or newcomer use the server's refusal codes.

## 8. D-057 asset safety (added 2026-10-18 at FORGE-00's request; priority behind #187 and the Crownfall gameplay PRs)
D-057 (`DECISIONS.md`, 64e71fde) brings licensed prebuilt assets and imported meshes into FL-0135 and FL-0136 and says Creator Store models are "vetted by FORGE-07 before use". The repo is public. Checks for each asset PR; none of them has been run yet because no such PR is open:
A1. **Licence from the primary source.** Each asset names its source URL and licence text in the manifest; I open the source page (or the pack's licence file) myself. CC0 only for anything committed; CC-BY needs its attribution recorded; any NC or ND licence, or "free for personal use", is refused. A paid pack (Synty or similar) needs the owner's recorded approval and is never committed.
A2. **Nothing non-redistributable in the tree.** List every new binary and archive (`fbx`, `glb`, `obj`, `rbxm`, `rbxmx`, `png`, `zip`) and match it to a manifest row with a CC0 licence file beside it; look for pack names, vendor folder names and embedded metadata of a paid pack; a Creator Store model must not exist as a committed file at all.
A3. **No code in an imported or Creator Store model.** For any `rbxm`/`rbxmx` or uploaded asset: no `Script`, `LocalScript` or `ModuleScript` instance, no `require` with a numeric asset id, and none of `loadstring`, `getfenv`/`setfenv`, `InsertService:LoadAsset`, `HttpService`, `MarketplaceService`, `TeleportService`, or obfuscated or base64 strings; check hidden children, `Sound`/`Decal`/`Texture` ids pointing at assets the manifest does not list, and attribute or tag payloads. A mesh format (`fbx`, `glb`, `obj`) cannot carry scripts, but its extension is checked against its content.
