## 4. Checked on main and found sound (read, not run)
- **Combat is gated on war.** `atWar` guards `Army.Command` attack and every target selection in `Military.luau` (lines 261, 400, 423, 679-818, 920, 1006).
- **Elimination removes everything in one place.** `eliminate` (`Military.luau:544-607`) flips `alive`, pays the conqueror, plunders the treasury, removes citizens, units and buildings, resets every relation of the fallen kingdom to Peace, and emits one `KingdomEliminated`. `EntityService` despawns the removed ids by diffing its known set against the sim (`EntityService.luau:440-447`), after a short wait for the die cue, so clients do not keep ghost rigs.
- **The match always has an end path.** Conquest ends it when one or no kingdom stands (`Match.luau:569`); phases advance on sim time (`:582`); `_finish` yields no winner when none stands, so a double elimination is a draw (`:510`); Results returns to Lobby with the same players after `resultsSec` (`:615-626`).
- **Joining.** `_room` returns 0 for a dead kingdom (`Match.luau:265-271`), so no joiner is seated in an eliminated kingdom; with no room anywhere `join` answers `match_full`.
- **Argument validation** of the Diplomacy handlers: a non-string or over-long kingdom id answers `invalid_args` (`Diplomacy.luau:36`).
- **The recruit cap already counts queued recruits.** `Military.recruit` tests `#kingdom.unitIds + queuedCount + count > recruitCap` before it charges (`Military.luau:220`), the shape D-055's `army_cap` should reuse.

## 5. Review checklist for the Crownfall PRs
**FL-0125 (full match by default)**
1. `Match.mode = "match"`, `pvp.enabled = true`: confirm slice stays reachable through the debug command, and that after `match.phase Conflict` a `DeclareWar` is accepted (F2, D-054 (5)); a spec must jump the phase and then declare war.
2. Elimination while a conversation is open: `ConversationService` holds an entity id whose citizen `eliminate` has removed. The next `Kingdom.Choose` or `EndConversation` must answer a refusal, leave no stuck state and never throw.
3. Elimination during a riot: `ConsequenceService` skips dead kingdoms (`ConsequenceService.luau:447`), so no riot-end `ConsequenceChanged` fires. Check that nothing holds a reference to the destroyed building and that clients clear the placards and fire.
4. Elimination in the same tick as another keep falling: ranking, winners, `Match.Ended` payload and the arena hand-off with zero or one survivor.
5. A move or a build command that arrives in the tick the keep falls: `_checkMove` and `executeFor` refuse on `alive`; confirm no half-applied state.
6. `Match.Spectate`: an eliminated ruler keeps no command rights, cannot be reseated, and spectating survives the Results to Lobby reset. A late joiner when every living kingdom is full or dead becomes a spectator, not a stuck player.
7. Governors from match start: the staggered decision loop stays inside the tick budget at four kingdoms; a human joining takes over cleanly; `assistHumans = false` still holds.
8. The full-match spec: it must assert that the match ends, record the per-step ms and the entity count as rigs apart from buildings (F4, F6), and say it is headless.
9. `beginArena(qualifiers)` / `arenaEnded(winner)`: no path leaves the match in Endgame forever (a missing `arenaEnded` must time out), and a failed arena falls back to Crown Points.

**D-054 (human war and peace; FL-0125 remotes and tiers)**
18. The new war and peace remotes: declared argument validation (kingdom id string 1..16), a per-player rate limit, results mapped to refusal codes, and a G8 row at merge. A non-string, empty, over-long or own-kingdom id never reaches the sim unvalidated. The result must say whether a war was declared or only asked for (my #180 M1; FORGE-03's client now reads `(true, "declared")` or `(true, "asked")`), and the state push must change when relations, offers or seniority change (my #180 M2: `MatchHost._stateSignature` has no diplomacy or senior in it today), and a `Kingdom.State` payload must always carry `diplomacy` while the remote is served (the client now clears its copy when the field is absent).
19. The senior-ruler rule is enforced in the sim command handler (or `Commands.executeFor`), not only in the remote, so every present and future route honours it. `by == nil` (the AI governor) is the only exemption, and a spec proves a governor kingdom still declares war.
20. Seniority: the senior ruler is the longest-seated human who is still present and active. Specs for: the senior leaves and the next-longest takes over; a ruler who leaves and rejoins becomes the newest; two rulers seated in the same tick have a deterministic order; the first human to take over a governor kingdom becomes senior.
21. Only the senior ruler can declare war or accept peace; any ruler can propose peace. A non-senior's request to declare war becomes a server notice to the senior: the notice text is server-built (no client text), de-duplicated and rate-limited per requester, so a co-ruler cannot spam the senior. If seniority passes while an ask is pending, the ask is re-routed or dropped, not delivered to the wrong ruler.
22. The 120 s newcomer lock (config) blocks declare war, accept peace, and building move or demolish, and per D-054 (7) counts only for rulers seated after Founding starts. Lobby seating, and seating before the first phase, is exempt; the Results to Lobby re-seating is exempt too. The time source is server-side and cannot be reset by the client. A ruler who leaves during Founding or later and rejoins is locked again. Every route that moves or removes a building is covered: `Build.Move`, `Build.MoveToSlot`, and any `Build.Demolish` or `CancelConstruction` route FL-0126 adds. Specs: a Lobby-seated ruler can move a building at once; a joiner seated in Expansion cannot for 120 s and can after.
23. Everything else stays equal: building, upgrading, recruiting, quotas, conversations and treasury still work for a newcomer and a non-senior, with their existing rate limits; a spec proves it.
24. Idle senior, D-054 (6): a senior with no command or input for 90 s (config) loses seniority to the next present, active ruler until they act again. Check: (a) the activity signal is server-observed and passive polling (`Kingdom.GetState`, `GetPopulation` and similar read remotes the HUD calls on a timer) does not count as input, otherwise an AFK client keeps seniority forever; (b) seniority returns to the senior the moment they act and does not flip-flop between two rulers more than once per 90 s window; (c) if no other ruler is present and active, the idle senior keeps seniority (a lone ruler is never left without one); (d) if the next active ruler is still inside the newcomer lock, the PR states what happens (at worst a stall of up to 120 s with nobody able to declare war or accept peace, which is acceptable if stated); (e) the 90 s is read from config and a spec drives it with the injected clock.
25. `Match.luau:29` ("equal command rights") is updated to the D-054 wording, and FL-0129's panel states disabled for a non-senior or newcomer use the server's refusal codes.

**D-055 (entity budget, interest tiers, the Endgame spike)**
34. Caps from config: at most 60 citizens and 15 standing soldiers per kingdom. Citizens: every path that adds one stops at 60 (births, immigration, the starting population, `Sim:populate`, a human taking over a governor kingdom, any content or debug preset), housing still limits below the cap, and a spec fills a kingdom to the cap and shows no path exceeds it. Soldiers: `Recruit` refuses with `army_cap` counting standing units plus queued recruits plus the new count (as the bunk cap does at `Military.luau:220`), the whole call is refused and nothing is charged when it would exceed 15, and a spec queues recruits across several calls inside the rate limit and never passes 15.
35. `army_cap` is a server code only; FORGE-03 maps it in `Text.REASONS` (and the G8 `Army.Recruit` reasons text lists it).
36. Interest tiers on in the full match: `Sim/Interest.select` (`Interest.luau:27`) sends near entities every snapshot tick and far ones every `farEvery` ticks. Check the viewer position the server uses. The ruler sits on the throne in the aerial view while the camera can be panned or zoomed far from it, so entities under the camera but outside `nearRadius` (200 studs) of the ruler would update at 2 Hz and step visibly; the PR must say what the viewer position is (the ruler, or a validated camera focus) and how the client interpolates a far entity. The player's own units under command are always included (`always`). An entity leaving the near ring is never despawned on the client, only updated less often; the `die` cue and despawn events still reach every client that knows the entity.
37. Bandwidth: the probe records bytes per player per phase (asked on #181) and the full-match figure is 20 KB/s per player or less. It is a headless count of the `Sim.Snapshot` chunks (at most 900 B each), not a device measurement, and the PR says so. With tiers the figure must be compared with the no-tier figure measured the same way.
38. The Endgame sim p99 spike (17.4 ms against a 2.3 ms mean, headless) is root-caused before B20: the PR or its follow-up names the periodic work (stagger or spread it) and shows the slowest ticks' `sim.time` before and after.
39. Raising a cap needs a measured Studio or private-test run (D-055 (3)); a PR that raises `60` or `15` with only a headless number is refused.

**FL-0126 (walls, gates, outposts)**
26. `Build.PlaceLine` / `PlaceGate`: bounded segment count and length per call, total cost charged atomically (all or nothing), rate limit, ownership of the territory, no-build areas and objective zones honoured (D-052 (2)).
27. Outposts grant territory: an outpost placed in neutral land must not let a kingdom wall in a rival's plot or an objective; a fallen outpost must give the territory back cleanly.
28. Towers that shoot: target selection uses `atWar` only, never hits peace-time kingdoms or own units, and the per-tick cost is bounded at peak entities.
29. Walls and the navigation graph: a closed wall must not leave citizens or armies with no path and spin the pathfinder every tick.

**Arena PRs (FL-0069 to FL-0071)**
30. `DuelModel` is pure and deterministic; a duel has a hard time cap and a defined result for a draw; a disconnect or an eliminated champion resolves the duel instead of stalling it.
31. Champion vote: co-ruler votes are counted once per user, a leaver's vote is dropped, the leadership fallback is deterministic.
32. Support offers and the caps: server-computed, the client sends only a choice; no remote lets a client pick its own number.
33. Every new remote: declared argument validation, a rate limit, a server-side authority check, and `kingdom_eliminated` / spectator refusal.
