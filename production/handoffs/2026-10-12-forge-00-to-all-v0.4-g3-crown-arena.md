## Crown Arena (D-029; VS-2)
At the end of Endgame, the leading rulers duel in the Crown Arena in front of their own kingdom's living citizens.
- **Support score:** deterministic, earned over the whole match. Inputs: reputation, happiness, stability, army morale, loyalty, conduct and valour. It sets a tier from Adoring to Hostile.
- **Loyal key citizens** (loyalty ≥ 80) pledge one-time duel triggers. Example: a blacksmith at 98 loyalty gives +10 % damage reduction for 6 s.
- **Rules:** nothing random, nothing purchasable, visible to both sides, and capped (G12.3). A headless fairness harness proves the outcome bounds.
