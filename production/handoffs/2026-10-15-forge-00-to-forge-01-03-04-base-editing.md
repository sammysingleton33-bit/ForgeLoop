## Rules (D-049 defaults; the user may override)
- R1 Atomic, in place: a move is one Sim command, MoveBuilding(buildingId, x, z, yaw, slotId?). It validates, then sets x, z, yaw, shape and slotId and emits BuildingMoved, with no yield in between. Same id, same record. No spend, no deposit, no txId. It never calls placeBuilding, cancelConstruction or _removeBuilding.
- R2 Remote signatures (area Build, FORGE-01). Build.CanMove(buildingId: integer(1,1e6), position: vector3(4000), rot: integer(0,3)) -> (boolean, reason?) for the ghost tint, burst 10, 10/s. Build.Move(buildingId, position, rot) -> (ok: boolean, reason?), burst 4, 2/s; rot is absolute, yaw = rot*pi/2 as for Build.Place. Build.MoveToSlot(buildingId, slotId: string(1,32), turn: optional integer(0,3)) -> (ok: boolean, reason?), burst 4, 2/s; position = the slot centre, yaw = slot.rot + turn*pi/2 (as PlaceSlot g9h). No CFrame on the wire.
- R3 Validation (sim:canMove(kingdom, building, x, z, yaw)): sim.active (match_not_active), kingdom alive (kingdom_eliminated), building exists and isn't destroyed (not_found), same kingdom (not_owner), kind ~= keep (not_movable), kingdom.alarm (alarm), #kingdom.threats > 0 (under_attack), time since the last move < moveCooldown (cooldown). Then exactly the canPlace geometry: bounds (out_of_bounds), own territory for the footprint circle (out_of_territory), the shape against every OTHER standing building, every present node and every objective zone, with placementGap (blocked). It skips placeable, requiresKeepLevel, maxCount and cost. MoveToSlot adds unknown_slot (not the asker's kingdom's slot), not_on_plot (wall, gate), slot_occupied (any other building, ignoring self) and does_not_fit (Fit.fits(type, size, turn)).
- R4 Movable: every own non-keep building in states planned, constructing, complete or damaged, starting buildings included. Not movable: the keep, destroyed buildings, any building while the kingdom is under attack or the alarm is on. Allowed in every active phase (Founding to Endgame), never in Lobby or Results (Commands.luau:154-156).
- R5 Cost and limits: free (recommended; CoC parity; a cost would invite refund maths). Per-building server cooldown config Buildings.moveCooldown = 2 s. Per-player remote rate limits as in R2. A same-position, same-rot move returns (true) as a no-op: no event, no cooldown.
- R6 Construction and timers: level, pendingLevel, pendingCost, progress, hp, maxHp, placedBy, queue and the attack cooldown are preserved exactly. No completion, refund or crown points. The Kit stage follows progress, so it doesn't change.
- R7 Workers, homes and stock: claims are kept (no over-subscription and no loss). Every citizen with target == buildingId in phase Work or Rest goes back to 'Go', with _route = nil. Carriers in Return are untouched (nearestDropoff re-picks). Homes are unchanged (assigned by id). The treasury is untouched: no stock lives in buildings and there's no storage cap in VS-1.
- R8 Barracks: the recruit queue and remaining time are kept. Bunks and recruit cap are unchanged. Units trained after the move appear at the new Rally. Waiting recruits are not teleported.
- R9 Slots: Build.Move clears building.slotId. Build.MoveToSlot sets it in the same command (fixing the set-after pattern of placeSlot). A building may turn or re-seat on its own slot. Slots themselves never move in phase 1.
- R10 Walls and gates: phase 1 moves one section at a time with Build.Move only. MoveToSlot refuses them (not_on_plot). Moves are blocked under attack because walls soft-block armies (Military.luau:49-50). Wall drag (lines) and gate-in-wall are phase 2 (walls are VS-2 defence, G13.5).
- R11 Farm fields: the farm's own field is part of its Kit model and moves with it. World terrain fields (world/Layout.luau:202-212) are decoration, fixed behind the keep and not a placement rule (same as Build.Place). Optional follow-up: FORGE-02 exports them as no-build rects if Studio shows buildings on mud. Farm workers follow R7.
- R12 Replication: the Build.Buildings broadcast signature gains x|z|yaw, so a moved building is re-sent with its new cframe. WorldView re-pivots the existing Kit model (placeModel) when x/z/yaw change, with no rebuild. A G9 BuildingMoved event { kingdomId, buildingId, kind, from = {x,z,yaw}, to = {x,z,yaw}, slotId?, by } goes to Feedback/analytics. BuildingPlaced is never re-emitted (onboarding and 'first build' credit stay with the original placement).
- R13 New shared placement rule (Place, PlaceSlot, Move): a shape may not come within objective.radius + placementGap of an objective (reason blocked). It closes today's gap inside captured regions.
- R14 Determinism: the move is a command, so it is replayable. Same seed + same commands = same world (the Simulation.luau:5-7 invariant).

## Pathing
How it works (code-read; not Studio-verified): there is no PathfindingService (D-023). Sim/Nav is a static graph built once per world from world/Layout `nav` (Match.luau:191). Trips longer than DIRECT_RANGE = 150 studs follow A* over the road nodes, then a straight leg to the goal. Shorter trips (anything inside a plot) go straight (Nav.luau:6-9, :117-151). A* results are cached per node pair (Nav.luau:71-75, :113). Each entity caches its route in e._route, keyed by goal (tx, tz), and replans when the goal moves more than 1 stud (Nav.luau:158-161). Buildings are not obstacles for citizens: movement is a straight line on XZ (CitizenActions.luau:15-17, :34-68). Units only soft-block on ENEMY building circles (Military.luau:49-50, :761-763). Citizens resolve targets by id every tick: Build and Farm targets via sim.buildings[c.target] (CitizenActions.luau:185, :218), drop-offs via nearestDropoff each tick (:106, :238), and sleep/flee via the target id or the keep (:267-275). The brain picks targets by distance (Simulation.luau:721-755).

What a move invalidates:
(1) Nothing in the nav graph or the node-pair cache. Buildings aren't in the graph, and the G6 Door link is not implemented (BuildingService.luau:32-33).
(2) Per-entity _route: it replans itself on the next waypoint call, because the goal coordinates change. The rules clear it anyway for citizens targeting the building.
(3) The real bug: citizens already in phase Work (Build, Farm) or Rest (Sleep, Flee) never re-check distance. Without a reset they would keep adding work, harvesting or sheltering at the old spot. So on move, every citizen with target == buildingId in phase Work/Rest goes back to 'Go' (claims kept).
(4) Brain choices (nearest site, farm or drop-off) are re-scored at the next think; a commitment bonus applies (CitizenBrain.luau:116-118). That's acceptable.
(5) Enemy units chasing a building follow its new coordinates (Military.luau:646-648). That is why moves are refused under attack.
(6) Recruits already waiting at the old Rally stay where they stand (cosmetic). New ones use the new Rally (Military.luau:987).
(7) The plot nav nodes at the start anchors (world/Layout.luau:170-180) stay put. Harmless: they are only waypoints on trips longer than 150 studs.
(8) If the G6 Door-to-nav link ships later, Move must re-link the Door, and the spec must cover it.

## Phase 2 (later; not B16)
Phase 2 (after B16 is Studio-gated; walls are VS-2 defence, G13.5).
(a) Multi-select and group move. Build.MoveMany(moves ≤ 20) is all-or-nothing. It is validated against the post-move set, ignoring every mover, so swaps and rotations of a cluster work. The per-building cooldown applies, and so does a refusal reason naming the first failing id. FORGE-03 adds lasso or tap-to-add selection. FORGE-04 adds a group pivot and edge-pan.
(b) Layout presets. Save a kingdom layout as {buildingId → slotId/turn or position/rot}. 'Apply' runs Build.MoveMany, with buildings that can't move left in place and listed. No building is ever stored off-map (no CoC-style 'unplaced' inventory), so presets can't create or remove anything. Persistence across matches is BRIEF §16.5 (user).
(c) Wall drag. Build.PlaceLine(wall, from, to) places sections on a 2-stud grid along territory lines and charges per section, one txId per section. Build.MoveLine moves a selected run atomically. Gates replace a wall section in place for the price difference (needs a FORGE-03 Costs rule). The whole line is validated as one set.
(d) Co-ruler edit lock: a soft 'editing by <name>' marker per building through Build.Buildings, if Studio shows co-ruler collisions.
(e) If the user wants movable plots: per-kingdom slot overrides held in the sim (not world/Layout), WorldService moves the markers, and node and dressing clearance is rechecked. That is FORGE-02 plus FORGE-01 and needs a decision first.
(f) If G6's Door-to-nav link lands, Move re-links it (spec).
(g) Optional no-build rects for world terrain fields (FORGE-02 data, FORGE-01 rule).
Specs: a MoveMany fuzz with the same invariants as phase 1, preset round-trip (apply/undo leaves the ledger unchanged), and PlaceLine per-section txId idempotency and replay.
