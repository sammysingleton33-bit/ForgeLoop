## B. Citizens
**Recommended: rigid MeshPart segments on the existing Motor6D tree, animated procedurally through Motor6D.Transform on clients** ([perf](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization/improve), [Motor6D](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/Motor6D)).
- Mini Characters (measured): 723–876 tris on a 7-joint rig that mirrors our tree. Skinning is rigid (2 of 12 checked), so a 6-part split is lossless.
- Parts sharing MeshContent and TextureContent are instanced into one draw call.
- Skinned bones: portrait or hero characters only. LOD reportedly skips skinned meshes (likely), and skinned instancing is undocumented.
- EditableMesh: no. It needs an owner toggle, the client memory budget is unpublished, it doesn't replicate, and it is capped at 20k tris / 60k verts ([ref](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/EditableMesh)).
- Limits ([specs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/art/modeling/specifications)): 20k tris per mesh, ≤4 influences per vertex, frozen bones, one material per mesh.

**Budget (working figure, unmeasured):** ≤1,000 tris per citizen (body ≤900, tool ≤150). That is about 150k tris on screen (150) and 300k in total (300); today a citizen is about 132. No per-citizen textures, PBR or normal maps. Each kingdom gets one 512² palette (TextureID swap), or all share one atlas tinted with MeshPart.Color.

**Pipeline:** Blender export with embedded textures → Studio Importer → .rbxm → Rojo `$path`. Kenney's external texture files have been [reported colourless](https://itch.io/t/5208814/kenney-assests-not-uploading-in-color-on-roblox), hence the embedding.

## C. Owner actions (3 sittings, $0)
1. **Browser (15 min):** download the free zips from `kenney.nl/assets/{mini-characters,castle-kit,fantasy-town-kit,nature-kit,survival-kit}`, save https://quaternius.com/license.html as a PDF, and upload them all to the session. Our proxy blocks these hosts.
2. **Roblox settings (5 min):** confirm whether a user or a group owns the experience. Before any upload, enable [Asset Privacy](https://create.roblox.com/docs/projects/assets/privacy); it is not retroactive, and Open Use is irreversible. Keep "Allow Loading Third Party Assets" off.
3. **Studio (1 h):** import FORGE's files and export .rbxm per the handoff. Run the 300-citizen stress test on a real phone (Render Stats, FPS) and take aerial and portrait screenshots.

No purchases or API keys needed.

## D. Creator Store safety
- Default: don't use it. Our own exports contain no scripts.
- Approved exceptions, in a scratch place only:
  - check the script count;
  - set SandboxedInstanceMode = Experimental and Sandboxed = true;
  - delete all scripts;
  - search for `require(`, `loadstring`, `getfenv`, `LoadAsset`, `HttpService` and obfuscated or whitespace-padded code ([guide](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/third-party-vulnerabilities)).
- Popularity and verification aren't safety signals. No runtime loading, no scanner plugins.
- Never commit store content or meshes extracted from it.

## E. Risks and unverified items
1. 300-citizen performance is unmeasured, so every budget above is provisional.
2. Visuals are unrendered: clothing, portrait readability, and character scale (about 0.7 units vs 1-unit kits).
3. QAL scope over downloads after 2026-08-28.
4. Importer behaviour: .glb, external textures, meshes over 20k tris, multi-material splits.
5. Kenney licences were read only in mirrors, the zip hashes come from third parties, and the 2026-03-29 Mini Characters re-upload is unchecked.
6. That MeshPart.Color keeps instancing is a community report only; bilinear TextureID filtering may make palette colours bleed.
7. Roblox's terms for free store assets; Rojo-built MeshPart sizing.
